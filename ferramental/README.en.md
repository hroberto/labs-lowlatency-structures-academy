## Why here, and not in `scripts/`

`scripts/` is automation **the student runs**: recording the environment,
diagnosing the machine, building and running the suite. `ferramental/` is
infrastructure **the project runs against itself**. Mixing the two made the
distinction between "a tool for whoever is studying" and "a quality gate"
disappear from the index in the upstream project.
